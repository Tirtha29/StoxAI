# Section 80C - India

Section 80C of the Income Tax Act allows a deduction of up to INR 1,50,000
per financial year from taxable income. Eligible instruments include:

- ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) mutual funds - 3 year lock-in, market linked
- PPF (Public Provident Fund) - 15 year lock-in, fixed government-backed rate
- Life insurance premiums
- 5-year tax saving fixed deposits
- Employee Provident Fund (EPF) contributions
- Principal repayment on home loans

The deduction reduces taxable income directly, not the tax owed - the actual
saving depends on the investor's marginal tax slab. Investors already
close to the INR 1,50,000 limit see diminishing benefit from additional 80C
investment and may look at other sections (80D for health insurance, NPS
under 80CCD(1B)) instead.

ELSS is generally preferred among 80C options for investors comfortable with
market risk because it has the shortest lock-in period (3 years) of any
80C-eligible instrument.
