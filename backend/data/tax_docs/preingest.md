1. Tax-Saving Investments & Deductions (Section 80C to 80U)

1.1 Section 80C - General Investments & Expenses

• Instrument / Category: Public Provident Fund (PPF), Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS), National Savings Certificate (NSC), Tax-Saving Fixed Deposits (5-year), Life Insurance Premium, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), School/College Tuition Fees.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Up to ₹1,50,000 (₹1.5 Lakh) aggregate cap per financial year.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Employees (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: PPF passbook/statement, Mutual Fund Consolidated Account Statement (CAS), Life Insurance premium receipts, Sukanya Samriddhi passbook, 5-Year FD receipt from bank, and official school/college tuition fee receipts (for up to 2 children).

1.2 Section 80C - Employee Provident Fund (EPF) / Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF)

• Instrument / Category: EPF and VPF contributions deducted directly through salary payroll.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Included within the overall Section 80C aggregate limit of ₹1,50,000 (₹1.5 Lakh).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): More beneficial / applicable to Private Sector Employees and salaried workforce under the EPF Act.
• Required Documents & Details: Annual EPF Statement issued by EPFO or Form 16 (Part B) showing monthly employee contribution deducted by the employer.

1.3 Section 80CCD(1B) - National Pension System (NPS) - Self Contribution

• Instrument / Category: Voluntary individual investment in NPS Tier-I Account.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Additional deduction of up to ₹50,000 over and above the ₹1,50,000 Section 80C limit (Total effective deduction up to ₹2,00,000).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Employees (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: NPS Tier-I Transaction Statement or electronic contribution receipt issued by the Central Recordkeeping Agency (CRA - NSDL / KFintech / CAMS).

1.4 Section 80CCD(2) - National Pension System (NPS) - Employer Contribution

• Instrument / Category: Employer's contribution to Employee's NPS Tier-I account.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Up to 14% of Basic Salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Employees (Full parity achieved: 14% limit now applicable to both central/state government and private sector employees).
• Required Documents & Details: Form 16 (Part B) explicitly detailing employer NPS contribution under Chapter VI-A deductions.

1.5 Salary Standard Deduction

• Instrument / Category: Flat standard deduction allowed against salary and pension income.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: ₹50,000 under the Old Tax Regime; ₹75,000 under the New Tax Regime (from FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26 onwards).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Salaried Employees and Pensioners.
• Required Documents & Details: Form 16 issued by employer or Pension Disbursing Agency. Automatically computed and applied during tax filing; no external proof submission needed.

2. Housing, Rental & Property Documentation

2.1 Section 10(13A) - House Rent Allowance (HRA) Exemption

• Instrument / Category: Exemption for rental expenses incurred for residential accommodation.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Minimum of: (a) Actual HRA received, (b) Rent paid minus 10% of Basic Salary + DA, or (c) 50% of Basic Salary for Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) / 40% of Basic Salary for Non-Metro cities.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Salaried Government and Private employees (Yields higher tax-saving utility for Private Sector employees who usually have larger HRA CTC components).
• Required Documents & Details: Signed Rent/Lease Agreement, original monthly Rent Receipts (with revenue stamps if cash > ₹5,000/month), and Landlord's PAN card copy (mandatory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000).

2.2 Section 24(b) - Home Loan Interest Deduction

• Instrument / Category: Interest paid on home loan borrowed for purchase, construction, or repair of residential property.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Up to ₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh) for Self-Occupied property; Actual full interest paid without upper limit for Let-Out / Rented properties.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Homeowners (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Annual Home Loan Interest Certificate issued by lending bank or housing finance company (HFC) explicitly segregating principal and interest paid during the year.

2.3 Section 80EEA - Additional Interest on Affordable Housing Loan

• Instrument / Category: Additional deduction on interest paid for first-time home buyers.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Additional deduction up to ₹1,50,000 (₹1.5 Lakh) over and above the Section 24(b) limit of ₹2 Lakh.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector First-Time Homebuyers.
• Required Documents & Details: Home Loan Interest Certificate from bank and property stamp duty valuation certificate confirming property value does not exceed ₹45 Lakh.

3. Health and Medical Deductions

3.1 Section 80D - Health Insurance Premium & Preventive Health Check-Up

• Instrument / Category: Medical insurance (Mediclaim) premiums paid for self, spouse, children, and parents.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Up to ₹25,000 for self/family (< 60 years); Additional ₹25,000 for non-senior parents OR ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents (Maximum cumulative deduction up to ₹1,00,000). Includes a sub-limit of ₹5,000 for preventive health check-ups.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Individuals (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Health insurance policy schedule and payment receipts (paid via non-cash mode; check-ups allowed in cash).

3.2 Section 80DDB - Medical Treatment for Specified Critical Diseases

• Instrument / Category: Medical treatment expenses incurred for specified critical illnesses (e.g., Cancer, Neurological diseases, Renal failure).
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Up to ₹40,000 for non-senior individuals; Up to ₹1,00,000 for senior citizen dependents or self.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Individuals (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Treatment prescription and specialized medical certificate issued by a specialist doctor (Oncologist, Neurologist, Urologist, etc.).

3.3 Section 80DD & Section 80U - Disability & Dependent Disability Maintenance

• Instrument / Category: Medical treatment, rehabilitation, or maintenance of a dependent with disability (80DD) or self with disability (80U).
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Flat deduction of ₹75,000 for standard disability (40% to 79%); Flat deduction of ₹1,25,000 for severe disability (80% or more).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Taxpayers (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Official Disability Certificate issued by a recognized Medical Board / Chief Medical Officer in Form No. 10-IA.

4. Higher Education Loans & Charitable Donations

4.1 Section 80E - Interest on Higher Education Loan

• Instrument / Category: Interest paid on loan taken for higher education of self, spouse, children, or a student for whom the individual is legal guardian.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: No upper monetary cap (Full actual interest paid is deductible for up to 8 consecutive financial years).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Taxpayers (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Education Loan Interest Certificate issued by approved bank or financial institution segregating principal and interest.

4.2 Section 80G - Charitable Donations & Relief Funds

• Instrument / Category: Contributions made to notified charitable organizations, government relief funds, and trusts.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: 50% or 100% of donation amount depending on fund classification (subject to 10% Adjusted Gross Income cap in certain cases; cash donations capped at ₹2,000).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Both Government and Private Sector Taxpayers (Universal applicability).
• Required Documents & Details: Official Stamped Donation Receipt showing Trust Name, Address, PAN, 80G Registration Number, and official Form 10BE certificate issued by the donee organization.

5. Income Reporting & Tax Credit Matching Documents

5.1 Form 16 (Part A and Part B)

• Instrument / Category: Annual Salary and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Certificate.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Covers complete salary breakdown, exemptions under Section 10, Chapter VI-A deductions, and TDS deducted and deposited under Section 203.
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Issued by Employer (Both Government departments and Private corporate employers).
• Required Documents & Details: Form 16 Part A (TRACES downloaded) and Part B (Employer generated salary breakup).

5.2 Form 26AS, Annual Information Statement (AIS) & Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS)

• Instrument / Category: Consolidated Annual Tax Statement and financial transaction profile.
• Max Deduction Limit / Income Range: Reflects total TDS/TCS credits, advance tax paid, self-assessment tax, dividend income, savings interest, and high-value financial transactions (SFT).
• Beneficiary Scope (Govt vs Private): Issued / Downloadable from Income Tax Department e-Filing Portal.
• Required Documents & Details: Form 26AS PDF / AIS JSON or PDF downloaded directly from the Income Tax e-Filing Portal.
